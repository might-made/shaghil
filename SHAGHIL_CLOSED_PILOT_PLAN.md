# SHAGHIL — Closed Pilot Plan

**Status: DAY 1A FOUNDATION — FOUNDER REVIEW REQUIRED**
**Mode: Documentation-only. Defines the plan; does not launch or recruit anyone.**

This document operationalizes §08–§09 of `SHAGHIL_NATIONAL_PRODUCT_READINESS_MASTER.md`. It does not authorize the pilot to begin — Founder approval of this plan is required first.

---

## 1. Wave 01 Structure

- **Size:** 10 real users, real businesses, real tasks.
- **Composition:** 3 SME founders/business owners, 2 e-commerce operators, 2 marketing/brand professionals, 2 content/social specialists, 1 agency/freelancer.
- **Duration:** Days 3–5 of the 14-Day Sprint (see Master §09), with evidence review on Days 6–7.
- **Constraints:** No payment collected. No public announcement or open sign-up. No claim of product-market fit made during or immediately after this wave — the wave exists to produce evidence, not to confirm a conclusion in advance.

## 2. Recruitment Criteria

Each participant must:
- Be a real Saudi SME or serve Saudi SMEs directly (for the agency/freelancer slot).
- Have at least one real, current marketing/content/communication need SHAGHIL's engines can plausibly address (not a hypothetical or test business).
- Be willing to complete real tasks with real business data (with the in-app local-data disclosure already presented — see Master §06).
- Be willing to provide structured feedback (the Pilot Scorecard, `SHAGHIL_PILOT_SCORECARD.md`) and, separately and explicitly, be asked for testimonial permission — never assumed.
- Be reachable for a short follow-up after initial usage.

Explicitly avoid: participants recruited only because they are personally close to the team (friendliness bias distorts evidence) — prefer real SME operators with a genuine, current need, even if that means slower recruitment.

## 3. Onboarding Sequence

1. Brief context-setting: what SHAGHIL is, what it is not (see Master §01), and that this is an early, closed pilot — not a finished commercial product.
2. In-app setup: the existing quick-start Business Brain flow (3 required fields) — no change to this flow.
3. A short, real first task chosen by the participant from their own actual current need (not a scripted demo task) — this is what "real-task testing" means in this plan.
4. Optional: guided walkthrough of Product Library and Visual Studio if the participant's task involves product visuals.
5. Explain the export/import workspace feature as their means of keeping their own data (consistent with the browser-local-only architecture, Master §04C/§06).

## 4. Real-Task Testing Method

- The task must be one the participant would genuinely do for their business this week — not an assigned demo scenario.
- Observe (or ask the participant to self-report via the Scorecard) which engine(s) they reach for, how many attempts a task takes, and whether the output was usable with or without further manual editing.
- Do not lead the participant toward any specific engine or workflow — the point is to observe organic usage, which is itself evidence (Master §07).

## 5. Feedback Method

- Primary instrument: `SHAGHIL_PILOT_SCORECARD.md`, one per participant.
- Collected via short structured conversation (call, message, or in-person) rather than an unattended form, to capture qualitative nuance (Master §07's "failure/confusion points").
- Saudi Relevance Score (1–5) is asked directly and verbatim, per Master §07.
- Testimonial permission is asked as its own explicit, separate question — never inferred from general positive feedback.

## 6. Pilot Support Process

- A single, clearly identified point of contact for all 10 Wave 01 participants during the pilot window.
- Response expectation: same-day acknowledgment of any reported issue during the active pilot days (3–5).
- Every reported issue is logged (informally at this stage; formalized into the backlog per §7 below if it recurs or blocks usage).

## 7. P0 Escalation Process

- Any issue that **prevents or seriously breaks real usage** (P0, per Master §10) is escalated immediately to the Founder — not batched for end-of-wave review.
- A P0 during Wave 01 pauses further onboarding of new participants until either fixed (through the full USER→DATA→DECISION→BUILD→QA→FOUNDER APPROVAL→RELEASE loop, Master §10) or explicitly accepted as a known limitation by the Founder for the remainder of the wave.
- Every P0 is recorded in `SHAGHIL_NATIONAL_READINESS_BACKLOG.md` regardless of whether it is fixed immediately, with its real evidence source (which participant, what task, what happened).

## 8. Wave 02 Entry Criteria

Wave 02 (target 20–30 users, Days 8–10) begins only after:
1. Wave 01 evidence review is complete (Days 6–7).
2. All Wave 01 P0 issues are resolved or explicitly Founder-accepted as known limitations.
3. The Founder has reviewed the aggregated Wave 01 Scorecards and Saudi Relevance Scores and approved proceeding.
4. Any P1 fixes the Founder prioritized from Wave 01 evidence have gone through the full development loop (Master §10) before Wave 02 onboarding begins, or the Founder has explicitly accepted deferring them.

No Wave 02 recruitment begins on assumption that Wave 01 "went fine" — it begins on a Founder decision informed by the actual evidence collected.
