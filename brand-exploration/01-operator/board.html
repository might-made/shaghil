A. Primary Arabic Wordmark

شغّل
شغّل
Small size (~24px row height): شغّل

Set in Noto Kufi Arabic — a geometric Kufi-derived face with even stroke weight and squared terminals. The ش's three dots and the غ's descender read as separated, deliberate geometric strokes rather than a calligraphic flourish; the shadda on the ّل sits as a small, crisp mark rather than a decorative swash. At small sizes the geometric evenness keeps the word legible as a whole shape before individual letters resolve — the intended reading order for a control-panel label.

B. English Wordmark

SHAGHIL

Space Grotesk, all-caps, tight tracking — reads like an engraved panel label, deliberately smaller and lighter in visual weight than the Arabic wordmark wherever both appear together. Arabic remains primary.

C. Symbol / Mark

A base bar (ش's base stroke) with three vertical ticks above it (ش's three dots, reinterpreted as a 3-position indicator) — the center tick lit in the brand ember. Reads as a toggle/gauge, not a letter, at a glance; resolves to ش on closer inspection.

Why this pairing: Noto Kufi Arabic gives display text a geometric, engineered character with no warmth to spare — exactly the register this direction needs at headline size. IBM Plex Sans Arabic carries body and UI text because Kufi faces lose legibility at small paragraph sizes; Plex's even x-height and open counters keep dense Arabic UI copy readable at 13–15px. Space Grotesk and IBM Plex Sans are Latin siblings from the same "engineered sans" family, so English never feels like an unrelated import.

J. "by MIGHT MADE" Lockup

شغّل by MIGHT MADE

"by MIGHT MADE" set as plain text only — small, muted-gray, uppercase, tracked — never as a reconstructed MIGHT MADE mark. No attempt is made here to recreate MIGHT MADE's locked Primary Signature; the credit is legible and credible purely through restrained typography.

L. Motion Principle — Snap

State changes are instant, stepped transitions — like a switch flipping, not an organic ease. Loading uses a fixed-rate spinner (no easing curve) and status text updates in discrete ticks rather than a smooth fade. No bounce, no overshoot.
