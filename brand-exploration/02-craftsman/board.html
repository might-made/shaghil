A. Primary Arabic Wordmark

شغّل
شغّل
Small size (~24px row height): شغّل

Set in Almarai — a warm, humanist Arabic face with slightly rounded terminals and organic curvature, still fully modern with no calligraphic flourish. The ش's three dots sit close and rounded rather than sharp-edged; the غ's descender curves gently; the shadda reads as a soft accent rather than a technical mark. The word feels considered and hand-finished without ever becoming decorative or script-like.

B. English Wordmark

Shaghil

Manrope, sentence case, normal tracking — reads considered rather than shouted, matching the wordmark's warmth. Set noticeably smaller and lighter-weight than the Arabic wordmark wherever paired.

C. Symbol / Mark

A single confident curved stroke (ش's base, drawn like one continuous brush pass) with one bold dot above it — ش's usual three dots deliberately simplified to one, the economy of a real hand-stamped seal, not a rendering shortcut — inside a circular seal frame.

Why this pairing: Almarai is a single Saudi-designed Arabic family confident enough to carry display, body, and UI text without a second Arabic face — its warmth and even weight distribution keep long Arabic paragraphs (content plans, offer copy) comfortable to read, which matters for a product whose entire output is Arabic text. Manrope is a Latin humanist sans of comparable warmth and x-height, so the two never feel like they come from different design eras. (Note: the brand strategy document named "General Sans" for this direction's Latin face; Manrope is substituted here for reliable free-CDN delivery in this exploration — see the accompanying markdown for this disclosure.)

L. Motion Principle — Settle

Transitions ease out gently, like something being placed down with care — no snap, no bounce. Loading uses a slow, warm pulse rather than a spinning wheel; results fade and settle into place rather than appearing instantly.
