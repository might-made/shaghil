# SHAGHIL V0.7 — Creative Production System

Starting point: locked V0.6 `6a1064958930a1b8bec04a60de6344e0c1f75bff`. Work belongs only to `shaghil-v0.7`; no merge or Production promotion. V0.6 remains locked. Version: `0.7.0`.

## Milestone 1 — Product Library and Content cards

Products are separate from brand logo/reference assets. IndexedDB upgrades the existing database without replacing Brand Brain or visual history. Up to 12 products retain original PNG/JPEG/WebP Blobs (8MB maximum, 40 megapixel decode limit), a lightweight reference copy, name, description and exact/creative preference. Exact is the default. Editing/deleting library entries does not rewrite saved creatives.

Content items render as cards with a summary, full details and a direct design action; the exact source excerpt becomes the primary idea, with the entire result retained as secondary context. Unstructured results retain the previous fallback. Campaign/Offer and all six engines remain available.

Assets remain local to this browser and origin. Different Preview URLs do not share IndexedDB/localStorage. Clearing browser storage removes data. No cloud storage, vendor or dependency was added. Large images never enter localStorage.

Validation: complete `npm run qa` (V0.5, V0.6, M1) passes with simulated responses; no live generation.

## Milestone 2 — Product fidelity and controlled composition

Studio selects a saved product and defaults to its fidelity preference. Exact Product sends product metadata and a scene-only instruction; neither the original product pixels nor brand reference images go to the image model in this mode. The unmodified original product Blob is preserved in the creative snapshot and drawn by canvas over the separately generated background. Size, horizontal/vertical placement, logo visibility and local text edits reuse that background without an API request. Logo proportions and programmatic Arabic text remain intact.

Creative Product sends the lightweight product reference plus brand references to the image edit endpoint and explicitly permits interpretation. Its product pixels are part of the generated background; local product-layer positioning applies only to Exact Product.

No automatic background removal, relighting, perspective matching, realistic shadows or photorealistic integration is implemented. Opaque JPEG/PNG uploads retain their rectangular background; transparent PNG is recommended. Canvas scaling resamples pixels for the final output but does not generatively redraw the product. AI backgrounds can still contain unwanted objects and must be reviewed. Composition failures visibly warn that only a background was saved.

Validation: V0.5/V0.6/M1/M2 suites pass, including original Blob identity in compositor draws, preserved proportions, 27 format/size/horizontal-position combinations, original logo, separate scene, exact raster exclusion, Creative reference forwarding and zero new requests for local controls. No live image generation.
